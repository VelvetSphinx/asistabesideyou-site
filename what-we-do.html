What we do

Support, plain and simple.

No jargon, no barriers. Here is how we walk beside you.

1
Patient navigation
Trained patient navigators help you understand medical information, prepare for appointments, and find your way through the healthcare system.
2
Education & workshops
Programs, workshops, and plain-language materials about diagnosis, treatment, supportive care, and self-advocacy.
3
Community outreach
Awareness and resource programs in underserved communities, meeting women where they are.
4
Partnerships for better care
We work with healthcare providers, hospitals, and community organizations to improve support services and access to care.
5
Support for families
Non-medical emotional and educational support for patients and the people who love them.
