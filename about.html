About us

Sisterhood through every step.

A Sistah Beside You Inc. is a Massachusetts nonprofit created to uplift, empower, and walk alongside Black and Brown women through every step of their cancer journey — every diagnosis, every treatment, every milestone.

We exist because too many women in underserved communities face cancer without the support, information, and advocacy they deserve. We provide education, compassionate support, patient navigation, and community outreach to improve health outcomes and access to care.

We welcome every woman, every diagnosis, and every stage of the journey — patients, survivors, caregivers, and family members alike.
